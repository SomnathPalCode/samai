SamAI

Birthday & anniversary reminders, and on-device prescription scanning.

About

SamAI is a small personal Android app used to keep track of family birthdays and anniversaries, and to extract medicine details from photographs of prescriptions. It is a personal tool and is not published on the Play Store.

Privacy policy

What the app stores

Reminders (a name, a date, and an event type) are stored in the app's private storage on your own device. This data is never transmitted to a server operated by anyone else, because the app has no server component.

Google Drive backup

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app requests a single, narrowly scoped permission: access to a hidden, app-private folder inside your own Google Drive. The app can only read and write a backup file in that folder. It cannot see your documents, photos, email, or any other file in your Drive account.

Sign-in uses the OAuth 2.0 authorization flow with PKCE. The app stores the resulting refresh token encrypted under a key held in the Android Keystore, and that token is explicitly excluded from Android's automatic device backup. Signing out deletes it.

Prescription images

When you scan a prescription photo, the image is sent to Google's Gemini API to extract the text. The extracted text is stored locally on your device. Images are never uploaded to any other service.

What is not collected

  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising of any kind.
  • No user accounts on any server we operate.
  • No crash reporting or telemetry.
